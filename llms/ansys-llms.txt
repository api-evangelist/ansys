# Ansys Developer Portal

> Comprehensive Ansys developer portal providing API and SDK documentation, tutorials, sample code, app catalog, and technical guidance. Enabling developers and engineers to develop applications, discover and integrate solutions, automate and extend simulation workflows, and accelerate product design, analysis, testing, and digital engineering initiatives across industries.

## Main menu

- [Home](https://developer.synopsys.com/home.md): Ansys Developer Portal landing page
- [Documentation](https://developer.synopsys.com/docs.md): Searchable documentation for libraries, APIs, SDKs, and developer resources
- [Knowledge base](https://developer.synopsys.com/knowledge-base.md): Articles, how-tos, and technical notes
- [Forum](https://discuss.ansys.com/): Ansys developer community and support discussions
- [App catalog](https://developer.synopsys.com/app-catalog/catalog.md): Discover and publish Ansys-based applications
- [GitHub](https://github.com/ansys): Open-source Ansys projects, including PyAnsys libraries
